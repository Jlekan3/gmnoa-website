About GMNOA

A united voice for Ghana's merchant navy officers.

The Ghana Merchant Navy Officers' Association was established 1970 with 53+ years of experience. It brings together merchant navy officers and maritime professionals with a shared commitment to professional development, welfare, solidarity and the advancement of Ghana's maritime sector.

GMNOA provides a platform for members to connect, collaborate, develop professionally and contribute to the growth of the maritime profession.

Mission & Objectives

Professionalism, welfare and unity at sea and ashore.

GMNOA exists to represent and support merchant navy officers while promoting professional standards and contributing to the development of Ghana's maritime sector.

01 / OBJECTIVE

Promote Professionalism

Promote high professional standards, competence, discipline and ethical conduct among merchant navy officers.

02 / OBJECTIVE

Protect Members' Welfare

Support the welfare, interests and professional development of members while providing a strong platform for collective representation.

03 / OBJECTIVE

Build Professional Unity

Encourage unity, cooperation, fellowship and mutual support among merchant navy officers and maritime professionals.

04 / OBJECTIVE

Advance Maritime Development

Contribute to the growth and development of Ghana's maritime industry through knowledge sharing, engagement and professional advocacy.
